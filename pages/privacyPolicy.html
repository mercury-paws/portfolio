Privacy

This website

This portfolio serves static HTML, CSS, JavaScript, images, and public articles. It does not set analytics cookies or send form fields to a website database.

Contact and reviews

The contact form prepares an email locally in your browser. It sends nothing automatically and does not save the draft in browser storage. Opening your email app passes the draft to that app. You decide whether to send it; your email provider then handles delivery. Copying a message puts it on your device clipboard.

Published reviews

Reviews are added manually. A review may be published with your name only when you give permission. Your email address is not published. To request a correction or removal, contact the address below.

Hosting and external links

When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security. External project links, GitHub, LinkedIn, Instagram, and your email provider operate under their own privacy policies. No external fonts, analytics, or embedded social widgets are loaded by this site.
